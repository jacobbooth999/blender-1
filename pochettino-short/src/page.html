UNITED TURN TO POCHETTINO
Club appoints new head coach with the team third from bottom of the Premier League
NEW MAN IN CHARGE Mauricio Pochettino, pictured with the USA in March 2026. Photo: Bryan Berlin / CC BY-SA 4.0

Manchester United have appointed Mauricio Pochettino as head coach, a day after dismissing Michael Carrick with the season already in crisis.

The Argentine, who has led Tottenham, Paris Saint-Germain, Chelsea and the United States national team, signs a deal running for two and a half years and takes charge from November 18.

He inherits a squad short of confidence and a fixture list that offers little respite before the winter.

CARRICK SACKED
Michael Carrick, pictured during his playing days at United. Photo: Gordon Flood / CC BY 2.0

Michael Carrick has left Manchester United after presiding over the club's worst-ever Premier League start.

The decision comes less than six months into a two-year deal, with United third from bottom and the board unwilling to wait.

Results on the pitch and difficulties in the transfer market left the former midfielder with few allies by the time the international break arrived.
