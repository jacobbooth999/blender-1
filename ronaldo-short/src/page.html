A STAR IS BORN
A kit man's son arrives on the Portuguese island of Madeira
THE BOY FROM FUNCHAL Ronaldo, pictured at United in 2007. Photo: Sam & Emer / CC BY 2.0

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born in Funchal on February 5, 1985, the youngest of four children.

His father, José Dinis Aveiro, worked as a kit man at local club Andorinha, and named his son after the American president Ronald Reagan.

The family lived modestly in the hills above the city, where the boy spent every spare hour with a ball at his feet.

Manchester United have signed Cristiano Ronaldo, 18, days after he ran Manchester United ragged in a friendly to open Sporting's new stadium.

Sir Alex Ferguson moved quickly, agreeing a fee of £12.24 million and handing the teenager the No. 7 shirt.
