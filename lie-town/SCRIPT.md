# Lie Town — script

Style: `style-guides/blocky-what-if-shorts.md` (blocky Roblox-style characters, one-word pop captions, hard cuts
about every 1.4 s, bright saturated colours, a neck badge that shows each person's money).

Cast:
- **Sam:** the hero. Lime-green shirt with a patch, brown hair. His badge always reads $0.
- **The Mayor:** black suit, gold tie, top hat, gold chain. Smug. His badge reads $9,999,999.
- **Townsfolk:** a schoolkid (red), a teacher (blue), a husband (blue plaid) and his wife (pink), and extras.
